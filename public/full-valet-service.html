Full Valet Service by AutoShine

For those who seek a thorough cleaning and rejuvenation of their vehicle, our Full Valet service at AutoShine Warks offers the perfect solution. This service is designed to meticulously clean and restore your car’s interior and exterior, leaving it looking and feeling new.

What’s Included:

Exterior:

  • Hand Wash and Dry: We start with a meticulous hand wash using premium, pH-balanced products to remove all dirt, grime, and road contaminants. We then carefully dry your car with soft microfiber towels to prevent water spots.

  • Wheel and Tyre Treatment: Your wheels and tires receive special attention, with a deep clean to remove brake dust and road grime. We finish with a tyre dressing for a shiny, new appearance.

  • Bug and Tar Removal: Stubborn bug splatters and tar spots are meticulously removed, leaving your car’s surface smooth and clean.

  • Exterior Windows: All exterior glass is cleaned to a streak-free finish, enhancing visibility and the overall appearance of your vehicle.

Interior:

  • Full Vacuuming: We vacuum the entire interior, including seats, carpets, mats, and the boot, ensuring that every inch is free from dust, dirt, and debris.

  • Interior Surfaces: The dashboard, centre console, door panels, and all plastic surfaces are cleaned and dressed, leaving them spotless and rejuvenated.

  • Door Jambs: We thoroughly clean the door jambs, removing dirt and grease, and ensuring that even the hidden parts of your vehicle are spotless.

  • Interior Windows: All interior windows are cleaned to a crystal-clear finish, providing unobstructed views and a polished look.

  • Air Freshener: We complete the service with a fresh scent to leave your vehicle smelling clean and inviting.

Why Choose Our Full Valet?

  • Comprehensive Care: Our Full Valet service is perfect for maintaining your vehicle’s cleanliness and preserving its condition, making it look and feel new.

  • Attention to Detail: We focus on every detail, ensuring that both the interior and exterior of your car are impeccably clean.

  • Convenience: As a mobile service, we bring the Full Valet directly to you, whether at home or work, making it as convenient as possible.

Ideal for Regular Maintenance

Our Full Valet service is an excellent choice for those who want to keep their vehicle in top condition. It’s perfect for regular maintenance, ensuring that your car always looks its best, inside and out.
